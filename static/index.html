🚀 Rusty Server

Space Weather & Exoplanet Data Dashboard

A Rust-based REST API service for fetching, caching, and serving space weather data critical for satellite operations. This service integrates with NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, NASA DONKI, and NASA Exoplanet Archive to provide real-time and historical space weather information, solar flare data, and exoplanet discoveries.
